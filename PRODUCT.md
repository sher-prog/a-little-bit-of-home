# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Delegated: the user left the choice to me. Plain static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, because the product is a small personal collection.

## Users
A personal recipe collector: one person keeping their own recipes. They open it to find a recipe from their collection and cook from it.

## Product Purpose
Find and cook a recipe. Browse and search the collection, then follow a recipe clearly while cooking. Success is getting from "what shall I cook" to a recipe in hand quickly, and being able to follow it with messy hands.

## Positioning
A personal collection, not a public recipe site: the recipes are the user's own, so there are no ads, no ratings from strangers and no life-story preambles.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Browse, search or filter the collection, and open a recipe detail view.
- Recipe content is not yet supplied. Titles, ingredients, steps and photos are open decisions, and none may be fabricated as the user's real recipes. Clearly marked sample content is acceptable for building.
- Saving, meal planning and shopping lists were not chosen and are out of scope for now.
- Name: "a little bit of home". Recipes are the owner's childhood recipes from Durban, South Africa. Recipes live as JSON content files.
- Later it becomes a community where others add recipes tagged with their country. Every recipe therefore carries an author and a country now; accounts and submissions are not built.
- Every recipe has a dish photo plus a photo and a personal note per main step. Photos are supplied later, so placeholder slots are required.
- Adapted recipes carry a source credit with a link. The first recipe is adapted from eatmeerecipes.co.za (not copied; its photos are not used).

## Evidence on Hand
No recipes, photos or brand assets exist in the project yet.

## Product Principles
- The recipe is the product: finding it fast and reading it while cooking outrank decoration.
- Cooking-time legibility: text must be readable at arm's length, and the layout must work on a phone propped on a counter.
- Personal over public: it should feel like a kept collection.
